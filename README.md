### 🌷 About me

I'm a data and business analysis professional with *3 years at Accenture, working on a global pharmaceutical company's US commercial data platform. It's the system behind brand, field sales, and compliance reporting, so when the data is wrong, real decisions are wrong too. My favourite part of the job is finding out *why.

- 💊 I lead *gap and impact analysis* for territory alignment changes across four therapeutic areas
- 🔍 I traced *80+ missing-data alerts* on an audit compliance dashboard back to their root cause and fixed them at the source
- ⚡ I helped cut incident triage from *about 35 minutes to under 3* with a Gen AI incident-response automation
- 🤝 I work directly with business stakeholders, and I love turning a vague data problem into a clear answer
- 🎯 Currently exploring *Pharma Business Analyst* and functional BA roles
